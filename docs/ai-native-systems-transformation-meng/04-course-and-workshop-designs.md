# 04 Course And Workshop Designs

## Core Course Design Rule

Every core course must feed the studio spine. Each course produces methods, artifacts, or evidence that students use in their PBL challenge.

## Core Courses

### AI Systems Foundations

Purpose: build common language for ML, LLMs, agents, AI limitations, and responsible use.

Student outputs:

- AI system concept map.
- AI risk checklist.
- First AI work log entry.

### Systems Diagnosis And Transformation Mapping

Purpose: teach students to map systems before proposing solutions.

Student outputs:

- System boundary map.
- Stakeholder map.
- Bottleneck and root-cause analysis.
- Opportunity thesis.

### Professional Practice Foundations

Purpose: establish sponsor communication, teamwork, project documentation, and technical writing habits.

Student outputs:

- Sponsor interview protocol.
- Team working agreement.
- Decision record template.
- Project communication memo.

### AI Systems Integration

Purpose: teach students to design, evaluate, and govern AI-enabled workflows.

Student outputs:

- AI workflow architecture.
- Model or agent evaluation plan.
- Human-in-the-loop decision map.

### Trusted Data Collaboration And Governance

Purpose: connect data engineering basics with trust, governance, sovereignty, standards, compliance, incentives, cybersecurity awareness, and responsible use.

Depth note: the required course gives broad trust/governance framing plus practical data-system literacy. Technical depth in data engineering and privacy-preserving analytics is elective or project-specific.

Student outputs:

- Data landscape map.
- Data access plan.
- Data risk and governance memo.

### Implementation And Prototyping Methods

Purpose: teach disciplined prototyping across software/data, cyber-physical/process, and service/product contexts.

Student outputs:

- Prototype plan.
- Validation plan.
- Iteration log.

### Field Seminar

Purpose: support the Semester 3 co-op, residency, or equivalent high-contact field implementation phase by structuring evidence capture, reflection, sponsor communication, and risk review while students work in real implementation contexts.

Student outputs:

- Field implementation log.
- Sponsor communication update.
- Risk review memo.
- Field reflection on implementation constraints, decisions, and evidence.

### Human-AI Work Design

Purpose: redesign workflows, decision rights, roles, handoffs, accountability, adoption routines, and human factors around AI-enabled systems.

Student outputs:

- Current-state workflow.
- Future-state human-AI workflow.
- Adoption risk memo.

### Scale And Governance Seminar

Purpose: prepare students to move from prototype evidence to implementation recommendation, governance model, and scale plan.

Student outputs:

- Scale plan.
- Governance plan.
- Residual risk register.

## Electives / Excellence Areas

Students choose depth modules linked to differentiated excellence. These modules extend the common core without turning the program into a narrow AI, management, or disciplinary specialization.

### AI Systems Integration

Purpose: deepen student capability in agentic workflows, applied ML/LLM evaluation, AI product architecture, and MLOps/LLMOps for responsible AI-enabled systems.

Student outputs:

- Agentic workflow design.
- Applied ML/LLM evaluation report.
- AI product architecture memo.
- Deployment and governance checklist.

### Systems Diagnosis

Purpose: deepen student capability in systems modeling, operations analytics, digital twins or simulation, complexity, and resilience.

Student outputs:

- Systems model or simulation.
- Operations analytics brief.
- Complexity and resilience assessment.
- Transformation roadmap.

### Implementation/Prototyping

Purpose: deepen student capability in advanced prototyping, IoT or cyber-physical integration, product/service experimentation, and software engineering for transformation.

Student outputs:

- Advanced prototype artifact.
- Product or service experiment plan.
- Cyber-physical or process integration note where relevant.
- Software engineering and iteration record.

### Practical Transformation Leadership

Purpose: give students practical depth in project leadership, stakeholder communication, change coordination, adoption planning, and entrepreneurship basics without making business strategy, finance, or economics part of the required core.

Student outputs:

- Stakeholder communication plan.
- Change coordination brief.
- Adoption plan.
- Sponsor-facing implementation recommendation.

## Required Workshop Series

### AI Operating Discipline I

Topics:

- Prompting and task decomposition.
- Human verification of AI outputs.
- AI work log format.
- When not to use AI.

Output: first AI work log.

### Sponsor Communication And Field Interviewing

Topics:

- Sponsor expectation setting.
- Interview protocol.
- Field observation.
- Translating ambiguous sponsor language into project evidence needs.

Output: sponsor interview protocol or field interview memo.

### Evaluation Harness Design

Topics:

- Rubrics for AI output quality.
- Test cases for agentic workflows.
- Failure mode capture.
- Human review thresholds.

Output: project-specific evaluation harness.

### Prototype Review Clinic

Topics:

- Prototype evidence standards.
- Technical soundness review.
- User/workflow fit review.
- Operational/value logic review.
- Iteration priorities before sponsor feedback.

Output: prototype review memo or revised validation plan.

### Responsibility Foundations

Topics:

- Ethics.
- Safety.
- Privacy.
- Accountability.
- Affected stakeholders.
- Failure modes.

Output: responsibility memo draft.

### Data Access Negotiation

Topics:

- Data access expectations.
- Data minimization.
- Security.
- Partner trust.
- Compliance.

Output: data/access plan for sponsor review.

### Field Evidence Capture

Topics:

- Field note structure.
- Workflow observation evidence.
- Sponsor and user feedback capture.
- Implementation constraint logging.
- Evidence traceability for portfolio and defense.

Output: field evidence capture plan or field implementation log template.

### Risk Review

Topics:

- Technical, workflow, safety, privacy, and adoption risks.
- Failure modes.
- Affected stakeholders.
- Residual risk documentation.
- Escalation and mitigation decisions.

Output: risk review memo or residual risk register update.

### Sponsor Communication Clinic

Topics:

- Sponsor review preparation.
- Milestone update framing.
- Evidence-based project status.
- Handling ambiguous feedback.
- Converting sponsor feedback into next-step decisions.

Output: sponsor update memo or sponsor review plan.

### Portfolio Writing

Topics:

- Evidence selection.
- Reflection.
- Competency claims.
- Sponsor-facing storytelling.

Output: portfolio evidence map.

### Career Translation

Topics:

- Turning portfolio evidence into employer-facing role narratives.
- Turning project outcomes into employer-facing role narratives.
- Turning AI operating discipline into employer-facing role narratives.
- Turning sponsor feedback into employer-facing role narratives.

Output: employer-facing project story or role-fit statement.

### Defense Preparation

Topics:

- Competency defense structure.
- Evidence argument.
- Sponsor and faculty question handling.

Output: defense outline.
